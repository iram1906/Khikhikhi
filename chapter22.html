If You Ever Doubt Yourself.

Bhondu, listen.

There will be days when you doubt yourself. Days when things don't go the way you wanted. Days when you wonder whether you're doing enough.

Even if the whole world doubts you, I don't want you to doubt the fact that I believe in you.

I am genuinely grateful that you're in my life.

And I hope you never forget that.
