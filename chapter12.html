Chapter 12

How We Somehow Stayed.

That's one of the things I think about when I look at us.

We aren't a perfectly scripted story.

We have stupid arguments. We get annoyed. We misunderstand things. We can be ridiculously dramatic.

But there is also support. Honesty. Respect. The ability to talk again.

Maybe staying isn't about never having chaos. Maybe it's about still caring underneath it.
