Things I Hope We Do Someday.

I don't know exactly what the future looks like, but I have a ridiculous number of tiny hopes.

  • Take way too many pictures together.
  • Explore random places together.
  • Have stories that start with "remember when..."
  • Laugh about our old arguments.
  • Keep annoying each other.
  • Put flowers in your hair just because I can.
  • Make you my cute little girl for approximately five minutes. 😭
  • Keep choosing each other through ordinary days.

Nothing extravagant. Just a future with lots of little memories.
