Chapter 02

The New Girl

It was my first day in a new school, so obviously I was busy figuring out where I belonged.

You were simply one of the people around me. Nothing dramatic happened. No cinematic music. No sign from the universe.

Just a new girl, a classroom, a green jacket, and one very ordinary beginning.

Funny how the moments that change everything rarely announce themselves.
