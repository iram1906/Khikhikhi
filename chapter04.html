Chapter 04

Just Classmates.

For a long time, that's all we were.

Normal school conversations. Asking for notebooks. Talking about ordinary things. Being around each other without knowing where any of it was going.

If someone had told us back then that we'd eventually have an entire archive dedicated to our story, we'd probably have laughed.

We were just classmates. Apparently the universe had other plans.
