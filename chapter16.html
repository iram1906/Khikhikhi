Chapter 16

The Little Things.

I don't think the biggest parts of us are actually the loudest ones.

It's the tiny things.

Your random "cutieee" moments.

Your voice notes.

The way your tone softens when you talk to me.

Random eye contacts.

The gossip you somehow need from me.

The way you say what's actually in your heart.

Those things become memories without asking permission.
