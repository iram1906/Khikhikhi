The Green Jacket

November 2019. I had just joined AGS Hajin. New school, new people, new everything.

And somewhere in all of that was you.

You were wearing a green jacket.

I didn't know your name. I didn't know we'd eventually become friends. I definitely didn't know that years later I'd be making an entire website about you.

I just remember thinking: okay... he's cute. And somehow also a complete bhondu. 😭

And that was it. Just one ordinary school day that neither of us knew would become the opening scene.
