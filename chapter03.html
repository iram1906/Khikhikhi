Chapter 03

First Impressions

My first impression of you was basically a contradiction:

Cute... but also bhondu.

Which, honestly, has remained a surprisingly accurate description all these years later.

I had no idea that the person I barely knew then would eventually become someone whose messages I would wait for, whose voice notes would make me melt, and whose existence would become part of my everyday life.
