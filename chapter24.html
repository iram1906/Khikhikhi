The Future.

Maybe one day we'll look back at this website and laugh.

We'll remember how seriously we took tiny arguments, the stupid jokes, the random conversations, the weird phases, and the ridiculous amount of overthinking.

"Do you remember when we used to argue about THAT?"

And hopefully we'll laugh because we made it through all those tiny moments and collected enough memories to fill an entire lifetime.
